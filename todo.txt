uniqueVisitors summed across days is an upper bound, not a true unique count. This is a known limitation of the daily-HLL-bucket design, not a bug — a visitor who clicks on both June 1 and June 2 gets counted twice in a 30-day summary. If you ever need a true unique-over-range count, you'd PFMERGE the daily HLL keys in Redis before they get flushed/deleted, rather than summing the post-flush MongoDB numbers. Worth a comment in the code so a future you doesn't assume it's exact — I left one in analytics.service.js
